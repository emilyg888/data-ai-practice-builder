# Client Discovery Questions

## Business context
- What business outcome are we improving?
- Which process, decision, report or risk area is affected?

## Data context
- What source systems are involved?
- Who owns the data?
- What data quality issues are known?

## AI context
- What role should AI play?
- Is AI summarising, explaining, recommending, deciding or acting?

## Governance context
- What controls, approvals and evidence are required?
- What regulatory obligations apply?
